Frequently Asked Questions

Is NaamSetu free to use?

Yes. The core jap counter, mala tracking and analytics are completely free. Optional Pro features are available for those who want more.

Absolutely. We use encrypted storage and secure cloud sync. You can read exactly what we collect in our Privacy Policy.
