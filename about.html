The story behind NaamSetu

A digital bridge to consistent Naam Jap.

NaamSetu was created with one simple belief: technology should not pull us away from devotion — it can help us return to it with more consistency, awareness and community.

The Vision

Why “NaamSetu”?

“Setu” means bridge. NaamSetu is designed as a bridge between modern daily life and the timeless practice of Naam Jap.

In a world full of notifications, schedules and distractions, even a deeply valued spiritual practice can become inconsistent. NaamSetu brings Jap counting, mala progress, streaks, Aura, analytics and community into one focused experience.

The goal is not to replace traditional practice — it is to support the seeker in staying regular, mindful and connected.

“A small daily practice, repeated with devotion, can become a lifelong rhythm.”

Built for seekers

A companion for everyday sadhana.

Sangam

Together in devotion.

Sangam brings real-world group Jap into NaamSetu. A room is created when people are physically together, participants join through QR, and everyone chants as a group while keeping their own contribution visible.

It is designed to strengthen the feeling of collective practice without turning Sangam into a remote chat room or invite system.

Our Mission

Help more seekers stay consistent.

Our long-term mission is to help a large community of seekers make Naam Jap a steady part of everyday life — through simple tools, meaningful progress, social encouragement and a respectful spiritual experience.

Privacy & Trust

Your sadhana deserves thoughtful handling.

NaamSetu uses local storage and secure cloud services to keep supported progress synchronized and to power features such as accounts, analytics, Sangh and Sangam.

Local resilience

Supported Jap activity can be retained locally so temporary connectivity problems do not immediately interrupt your practice.

Secure cloud sync

Cloud services are used to synchronize supported data and enable account-based features across NaamSetu.

User control

NaamSetu provides account deletion inside the app, with additional help available through support when needed.

Built with devotion, not just features.

NaamSetu is being built step by step with the aim of creating a respectful, modern and useful space for Naam Jap — a place where technology supports the practice instead of becoming the focus.
