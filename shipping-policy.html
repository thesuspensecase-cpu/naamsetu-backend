Shipping & Spiritual Shop Policy

Last Updated: August 05, 2026

Feature Notice: Digital Platform Only

NaamSetu currently operates strictly as a digital application. The "Spiritual Shop" and physical merchandise shipping services are conceptual features provided for UI preview purposes only and are currently inactive.

1. The Spiritual Shop Concept

  • Current Status: The Spiritual Shop section inside the app represents a planned extension for physical spiritual goods (e.g., Jap Malas, Counter Rings, Prayer Mats, Divine Cards).
  • No Physical Sales Currently: At present, no physical products are offered, sold, or processed through this app. All items currently available in NaamSetu are purely digital assets delivered electronically.

2. Proposed Future Shipping Policy

In the event that physical merchandise shipping is officially launched in a future update, the following guidelines will apply:

  • Fulfillment & Delivery: Physical orders will be processed within 2–4 business days and dispatched via standard courier partners.
  • Shipping Rates & Timelines: Estimated shipping costs and delivery times will be displayed at checkout prior to payment.
  • Order Tracking: Tracking numbers and courier links will be sent via registered email upon dispatch.
  • Non-Guarantee Notice: NaamSetu makes no commitment or guarantee regarding the exact release date or availability of physical goods shipping.

3. Digital Item Delivery

  • Instant Provisioning: All digital goods (Coins, Aura Points, Divine Cards, Streak Savers) purchased via Google Play Billing are delivered instantly inside your app profile upon payment verification.
  • No Physical Dispatch: Digital items do not require physical packaging or postal delivery.
