Sangam

Jap together, when you are together.

Sangam is NaamSetu’s real-time group Jap experience. One person creates the room, nearby seekers join through QR, and the group chants together while individual and collective progress stays visible.

QR-only joining: designed for people who are physically together, not remote room requests.
Shared progress: see room Jap and your own contribution while Sangam is active.
Mala moments: complete 108-Jap milestones together.
Aura rewards: verified Sangam activity can contribute to your NaamSetu identity.

FAQ

A few quick answers.

Is NaamSetu free to use?

Yes. You can begin Naam Jap and use the core experience for free. Optional Premium features may be available inside the app.

Does Jap work if my connection is unstable?

NaamSetu is designed to preserve Jap locally and sync supported activity when connectivity is available. Some social, Sangam and cloud features require an internet connection.

How does Sangam work?

A user creates a Sangam room and nearby participants join by scanning its QR code. Sangam is designed for group Jap when participants are physically together.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Account deletion is available from within the app. You can also contact support@naamsetu.com if you need help.

How can I contact support?

Email support@naamsetu.com for account, app or subscription-related help.
