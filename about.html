The Vision

In the whirlwind of modern life, maintaining a consistent spiritual practice can often feel out of reach. NaamSetu was born from a simple yet profound vision: to build a "Setu" (Bridge) between our daily digital existence and our timeless spiritual roots. We believe that technology, when used mindfully, shouldn't be a distraction, but a powerful catalyst for devotion.

Crafted for Sadhakas

NaamSetu is not just an app; it is a companion for your spiritual journey. Every pixel and line of code is designed to enhance your "Naam-Jap" experience.

Digital Jap Counter

Experience an intuitive counting interface with haptic feedback that mimics the physical sensation of a Mala, allowing you to focus entirely on the chant.

Global Handy Circle

Our unique foreground overlay tool ensures your chanting never stops. Count your Japs seamlessly while reading, browsing, or working on your device.

Global Sangh

You are never alone in your Sadhana. Connect with a global community of seekers through our Global Sangh Leaderboard, fostering a spirit of healthy devotion and collective growth.

Divine Rewards

Stay motivated with our gamified ecosystem. Earn Aura Points, unlock Divine Cards, maintain Streaks, and use Boosters to reach new milestones in your spiritual progress.

Our Mission

Empowering 1 Million Seekers

Our mission is to empower a global community of seekers to build unwavering consistency in their Sadhana. By providing tools that track progress and provide deep insights through Sadhana Analytics, we help you turn sporadic efforts into a lifelong habit of devotion.

Privacy & Trust

We respect the sanctity of your spiritual data. NaamSetu follows an Offline-First Architecture. Your Jap counts and personal progress are stored securely on your device using a local Room Database. Cloud synchronization via Firebase is used strictly to ensure your data stays safe across devices and to power community features like the Leaderboard.
