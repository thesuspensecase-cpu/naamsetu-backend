Help & Support

Everything you need to know about your spiritual companion.

1. Getting Started

How do I start a Jap session?
Open the App, select your desired Mantra from the home screen, and tap the Jap button. Each tap counts as one chant. The counter will automatically track your progress towards a "Mala" (108 cycles).
What is the "Global Handy Circle" and how do I use it?
The Global Handy Circle is a floating overlay that lets you count Japs while using other apps. To enable it, you must grant the "Appear on top" (System Alert Window) permission in your device settings. Once enabled, you can minimize NaamSetu and keep chanting via the floating icon.

2. Account & Data Syncing

How does my data stay safe?
NaamSetu uses an Offline-First Architecture. Your Jap counts are saved instantly to your device's local database (Room DB). This ensures you never lose a chant even without internet. When you go online, these counts are seamlessly synced to our secure cloud (Firebase).
Can I use NaamSetu on multiple devices?
Yes! Simply sign in with the same Google or Email account on both devices. Your progress, streaks, and Divine Cards will sync automatically once you are online.
What happens if I uninstall the app?
If you are signed in, your data is backed up in the cloud. However, if you use the app as a guest, uninstalling will delete your local progress permanently. We recommend signing in to protect your Sadhana history.

3. Gamification & Features

How do Streaks and Streak Savers work?
Perform at least one Mala every day to maintain your streak. If you miss a day, your streak will reset unless you have a Streak Saver active. You can earn Savers through milestones or purchase them with Coins.
What are Aura Points and Divine Cards?
Aura Points reflect your spiritual consistency. As you chant, you earn Aura and Virtual Coins. These can be used to unlock Divine Cards—unique digital collectibles that represent different deities and spiritual levels.
How does the Global Sangh Leaderboard work?
The Leaderboard ranks users globally based on their total Jap count and Aura. It updates in real-time. To ensure fairness, we use Google Play Integrity and internal anti-cheat systems to detect and ban auto-clickers or automated scripts.

4. Troubleshooting

My Jap count isn't updating on the Leaderboard.
Ensure you have a stable internet connection. Sometimes it takes a few minutes for the sync to reflect on the global servers. If it persists, try restarting the app.
Ads are not loading or causing lag.
We use Google AdMob for monetization. If ads don't load, check your internet or ad-blocker settings. If the app freezes, please clear the app cache from Settings > Apps > NaamSetu > Storage.
I made a purchase but didn't receive the items.
Purchases are handled via Google Play Billing. If your items don't appear within 10 minutes, go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchases." If the issue continues, contact us with your Google Play transaction ID.
